Redaction vs. Highlighting: Know the Difference

This is a critical distinction that can compromise your privacy. A quick highlight is not the same as a secure redaction.

  • Highlighting is a temporary visual effect. The text remains in the document's code, so anyone can simply copy and paste it to reveal the information.
  • Redaction is the permanent removal of data. When you redact with TapToPDF, the selected content is completely deleted from the file, ensuring it can never be recovered.

For true privacy, always choose redaction to permanently blackout sensitive information like names, addresses, and account numbers.
