How to Unlock PDFs with TapToPDF

TapToPDF makes it easy and fast to remove passwords from your PDF files. Follow these simple steps to unlock your document in seconds, without needing any software installation.

1

Upload Your PDF

To begin, upload your password-protected PDF. You can do this by either dragging and dropping the file into the designated area or by clicking the "Choose File" button to select it from your device.

2

Enter the Password

After uploading, our tool will prompt you to enter the password for your PDF. Simply type in the correct password to allow our system to remove the protection. This step is required for secured PDFs. If the file is not protected, this step will be skipped.

3

Download Your Unlocked PDF

Once the process is complete, a download link will appear. Click the "Download Unlocked PDF" button to save the now password-free file to your device.
